# Availability and Services

## What he is open to

Ahmed is open to **internships**, **student collaborations** and **small freelance
tasks**.

He is available **part time alongside his studies**, and replies **within one day**
on email.

## What he can help with

### Python support

Scripts, automation and cleanup for class or side projects.

### Data basics

Collecting, cleaning and charting data with pandas for clear reports.

### ML starter work

Baseline models and honest evaluation with scikit-learn.

### Simple sites

Fast Astro pages for portfolios, clubs and events.

## Working process

Three steps, described as "three calm steps".

1. **Share the goal.** Send context, deadline and examples.
2. **Agree the scope.** He confirms the timeline and what is included.
3. **Build in the open.** Short updates and one clean handoff.

## Scope preference

He looks for small, well-defined work rather than large projects, and prefers
clear communication over large ongoing commitments.
