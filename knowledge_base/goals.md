# Goals

Ahmed's goals are to **build a future with AI**, have a **stable career**, and
**enjoy life**.

## The three goals

### Build a future with AI

He wants his future to be built with artificial intelligence as the foundation of
his work, rather than treating AI as a side interest or a single class topic.

### Have a stable career

He wants career stability: steady, sustainable professional work rather than
short-lived or unpredictable opportunities.

### Enjoy life

He wants to actually enjoy life, not only work toward the first two goals. This is
a deliberate goal in its own right, not an afterthought.

## How he intends to get there

- **Keep improving his technical skills.** Continuous deliberate improvement of
  his programming, machine learning and engineering ability.
- **Build meaningful projects.** Projects that are worth building and that move
  him forward, rather than filler work.
- **Balance his career with actually enjoying life.** He is explicit that the
  career and the enjoyment both matter, and that the second is not something to
  sacrifice to the first.

## How to treat these goals

- These are his own stated aims, not a plan with dates attached.
- Do not attach deadlines, milestones, salary figures, job titles or target
  employers to them. None of those are stated.
- Do not treat "stable career" as a promise of employment, and do not report it
  as one. He currently has no formal employment history on record.
