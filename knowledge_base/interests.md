# Personal Interests

These are personal, not professional. They describe how Ahmed spends time away
from study and project work, and are kept separate from his skills and employment
records.

## Technology

He is drawn to technology for its own sake, not only as a subject he has to
study. Building things that work locally on his own machine is a genuine
interest, not just a constraint.

## Anime and manga

He follows **anime** and reads **manga**. These are long-standing personal
interests.

## Gaming

**Gaming** is a personal interest and a regular pastime.

## Comic books

He reads **comic books**, alongside his anime and manga interest.

## Balance

He deliberately balances his projects with **enjoying life**. Building a large
number of projects is not the goal in itself; the goal is useful work that leaves
room for enjoyment. His stated preference is for solving small problems well over
racing toward large ambitious ones.

## How to treat these

- These interests are personal context, not professional credentials.
- Do not present them as skills, work experience or achievements.
- Do not invent specific titles, series, games, genres or creators he has not
  named.
- He has not listed any competitive gaming, esports or publishing activity.
